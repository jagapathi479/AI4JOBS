🎯 TIPS FOR SPOTTING FAKE JOB POSTINGS:

🚩 RED FLAGS:
  • Requests for upfront payment (training fees, materials, etc.)
  • Unrealistic salary promises ($500+/day for simple tasks)
  • Excessive exclamation marks and ALL CAPS text
  • Vague or generic company names
  • 'Too good to be true' claims (no experience needed, guaranteed income)
  • Urgency tactics (limited spots, act now)
  • No interview process mentioned
  • Poor grammar and unprofessional language

✓ SIGNS OF LEGITIMATE JOBS:
  • Specific job requirements and qualifications
  • Realistic salary ranges
  • Professional, detailed job descriptions
  • Clear company information
  • Standard application process
  • Benefits package mentioned
  • No upfront fees required
